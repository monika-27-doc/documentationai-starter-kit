<Update label="2026-10-10" description="Pro plan" tags={["plans","rate-limits"]}>
  ## What's new

  The new Pro plan provides 600 requests per minute, 250,000 requests per day, and 25 concurrent requests. It sits between the Free and Enterprise plans.

  ## Improved docs

  The [rate limits](/rate-limits), [plans and billing](/plans-and-billing), and [usage and quotas](/usage-and-quotas) pages now include the Pro tier in their limits tables.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-02" description="Schedule time zones" tags={["schedules"]}>
  ## What's new

  Schedules accept a `timezone`, so a `0 9 * * *` schedule runs at 9:00 local time all year, across daylight saving changes. See [Schedule jobs](/guides/schedules).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-15" description="Webhook signing" tags={["webhooks","security"]}>
  ## What's new

  Every webhook delivery now carries a `Kestrel-Signature` header, and each SDK has a `webhooks.verify` helper. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks).
</Update>
