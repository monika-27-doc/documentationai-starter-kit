---
title: Webhooks
---

Larkspur sends a `POST` request to your endpoint whenever a delivery changes status.

| Event | When it fires |
| --- | --- |
| `delivery.created` | A delivery is accepted |
| `delivery.picked_up` | The courier collects the parcel |
| `delivery.completed` | The parcel is handed over |

<Callout kind="tip">
  Verify the `Larkspur-Signature` header before trusting a payload.
</Callout>
