---
title: Is there a free plan?
description: What the Free plan includes and when to move to Pro.
---

Yes. Every workspace starts on the Free plan, with 60 requests per minute and 10,000 requests per day. It has every feature, including schedules and webhooks.

Move to Pro when you run production traffic or need more than 5 concurrent requests. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing).
