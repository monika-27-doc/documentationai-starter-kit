A `429 Too Many Requests` error means your workspace has used up its rate limit for the current minute or day.

## Check your plan's limits

<LimitsTable />

## Fix it

- Wait for the number of seconds in the `Retry-After` header, then retry.
- Retry with exponential backoff instead of a fixed delay.
- Check **Settings > Usage** to see which limit you reached.
- If you reach your limits often, upgrade your plan. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing).
