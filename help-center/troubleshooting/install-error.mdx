---
title: My jobs stay queued
description: Why jobs stay in the queued status, and how to get them running.
---

A job stays `queued` until a worker picks it up. If nothing moves:

1. Check that a worker is running and polling the same queue the job is in.
2. Check that the worker uses a key from the same environment. Test workers never see live jobs.
3. Open **Queues** in the app. If the queue is paused, click **Resume**.
