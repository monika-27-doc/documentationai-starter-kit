Every workspace starts on the Free plan, the default starting plan. Pro is for teams needing higher throughput, with limits of 600 requests per minute, 250,000 requests per day, and 25 concurrent requests. Enterprise offers custom limits through sales. Upgrade at any time from **Settings > Billing**. Changes take effect straight away, and you're charged the difference for the rest of the billing period.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing" icon="credit-card" title-type="p">
    Go to **Settings > Billing** and click **Change plan**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a plan" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
    Pick the plan you want. Your new limits apply as soon as the change is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Need limits above the Pro plan? Contact sales about Enterprise.
</Callout>
