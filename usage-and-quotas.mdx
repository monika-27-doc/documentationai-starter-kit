## Check your usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the usage page" icon="bar-chart-3" title-type="p">
    In the app, go to **Settings > Usage**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the meter" icon="gauge" title-type="p">
    The meter shows today's requests against your daily limit. It turns amber at 80% and red at 100%.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set an alert" icon="bell" title-type="p">
    Click **Alerts** to get an email when you reach 80% of any limit.
  </Step>
</Steps>
